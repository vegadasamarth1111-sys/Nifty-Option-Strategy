Expiry Straddle Sell
Sell ATM Call + Put on every Thursday. Collect maximum theta decay.
SELL Thursday Only Medium Risk

Strategy Logic

📅WhenEvery Thursday only (weekly expiry day)
⏰Entry9:20 AM — Sell ATM Straddle (Call + Put at same ATM strike)
💰ActionSell 1 ATM Call + Sell 1 ATM Put. You receive premium upfront.
🎯StrikeATM = nearest 50-point strike to Nifty spot price
🚪Exit2:30 PM — Buy back both options (close position)
🛑StopNone in backtest. In real trading: exit if loss = 2× premium received

Entry Time Timeline

9:15 Market opens → 9:20 ⬤ SELL → hold entire day → 2:30 ⬤ BUY BACK → 3:30 Expiry

Worked Example

Thursday, Nifty = 22,000
ATM Strike22,000
Sell 22000 CE @ 9:20 AM₹95/unit (receive)
Sell 22000 PE @ 9:20 AM₹90/unit (receive)
Total premium received₹185 × 65 = ₹12,025
Nifty closes at 22,050(small move)
Buy back CE @ 2:30 PM₹52 (decayed)
Buy back PE @ 2:30 PM₹10 (nearly zero)
Cost to close₹62 × 65 = ₹4,030
Net P&L (1 lot)+₹7,995 profit ✓

Pros & Cons

Pros
  • Highest win rate on expiry: 89%
  • Theta decays fastest near expiry
  • Only 1 trade per week (low effort)
  • Both options nearly worthless at 3:30PM
Cons
  • Big move on Thursday = large loss
  • Requires high margin (~₹1-2L/lot)
  • Only 52 trades/year
  • No stop-loss in basic version

Best overall performer. Sell ATM straddle every morning with a smart midday stop.
SELL Every Day Medium Risk

Strategy Logic

📅WhenEvery trading day — no filter, fully systematic
⏰Entry9:30 AM — Sell ATM Call + Sell ATM Put (straddle)
🛑Stop RuleCheck Nifty at 12:00 PM — if moved >0.6% from ATM strike → exit early
🚪Normal Exit2:30 PM — if no stop triggered, close at 2:30 PM
💡Why it works~85% of days Nifty stays within 0.6% of the ATM strike at noon. You collect theta decay on most days.

S14
Iron Condor Sell — Daily
Defined-risk premium collection. Sell OTM options, buy further OTM as hedge. Safest sell strategy.
SELL Every Day Defined Risk

Strategy Logic — 4 Legs

⏰Entry10:00 AM — after opening noise settles
📞Leg 1SELL Call at 0.5% OTM (e.g. 22,100 if Nifty=22,000) → receive premium
📞Leg 2BUY Call at 1.5% OTM (e.g. 22,350) → pay premium (hedge)
📉Leg 3SELL Put at 0.5% OTM (e.g. 21,900) → receive premium
📉Leg 4BUY Put at 1.5% OTM (e.g. 21,650) → pay premium (hedge)
💰Profit ZoneNifty stays between 21,900 and 22,100 (the short strikes)
🚪Exit2:30 PM — close all 4 legs
🛡️Max LossCapped at spread width minus net credit received (defined risk!)

P&L Diagram

Nifty = 22,000 at 10:00 AM
Sell 22100 CE+₹60 received
Buy 22350 CE-₹18 paid
Sell 21900 PE+₹58 received
Buy 21650 PE-₹20 paid
Net Credit₹80/unit = ₹5,200 (1 lot)
If Nifty stays 21,900–22,100+₹5,200 ✓
Max Loss (if blows through)-₹11,700 (limited)
🛡️ Unlike naked straddle sell, the Iron Condor has capped maximum loss. Even if Nifty crashes 5%, your loss is limited to the spread width. This is the safest sell strategy.

Pros & Cons

Pros
  • Defined maximum loss (safest)
  • 84.8% win rate — best of sell strats
  • No stop-loss needed (hedge does it)
  • Works in ranging, sideways markets
  • Lower margin vs naked straddle
Cons
  • Lower net credit (hedges cost money)
  • 4 legs = 4× transaction costs
  • OTM options have less theta vs ATM
  • Trending days blow through short strikes

Mid-Week Strangle Sell
Highest win rate: 96.1%. Sell slightly OTM options Tue/Wed/Thu when theta is high.
SELL Tue / Wed / Thu Medium Risk

Strategy Logic

📅WhenTuesday, Wednesday, Thursday only (mid-week, more theta remains)
⏰Entry9:30 AM — Sell 1% OTM Call + Sell 1% OTM Put
📐StrikesCall strike = 1% above spot | Put strike = 1% below spot
🛑Stop RuleCheck at 1:00 PM — if Nifty moved >1.2% from 9:30 level → exit early
🚪Normal Exit2:30 PM if no stop triggered
💡Why 1% OTM1% OTM gives wider safety buffer. Nifty must move >1% to touch your strikes.

Strangle vs Straddle

Strangle Sell vs Straddle Sell
Straddle (ATM)Higher premium, loses if any move
Strangle (1% OTM)Lower premium, larger safety zone
Example with Nifty = 22,000:
Sell 22,220 CE (1% OTM)₹35/unit received
Sell 21,780 PE (1% OTM)₹32/unit received
Total Credit₹67 × 65 = ₹4,355/lot
Breakeven Range21,713 to 22,287
Win if Nifty stays in range+₹4,355 ✓
⚠️ Unlike Iron Condor, the strangle has unlimited risk if Nifty moves far. The 1:00 PM stop-loss at 1.2% is essential to manage tail risk.

Pros & Cons

Pros
  • Highest win rate of all: 96.1%
  • Sharpe 14.64 — extremely consistent
  • 1% OTM = wide safety buffer
  • Stop-loss limits big losses
  • Only Mon/Tue/Wed/Thu (3 days/week)
Cons
  • Unlimited downside if stop fails
  • Trending days hit stops frequently
  • Lower premium than ATM straddle
  • Need 1 PM monitoring for stop

⚠️ Important Disclaimer & Risk Warning

This is a research/educational backtesting tool only. All results are based on synthetic option prices computed using the Black-Scholes model with historical spot data. Real option prices differ due to liquidity, bid-ask spreads, IV skew, and market impact.

Selling strategies carry unlimited risk. S13 (Daily Straddle Sell) and S15 (Strangle Sell) have unlimited downside on extreme market days (e.g., March 2020 COVID crash, budget announcements). A single bad day can erase weeks of profit.

Before trading with real money: (1) Paper trade for minimum 3 months. (2) Start with 1 lot only. (3) Always use hard stop-losses on broker platform. (4) Understand margin requirements — selling options requires ₹1–3 Lakhs margin per lot. (5) Consult a SEBI-registered financial advisor.
